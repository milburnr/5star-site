CertainTeed shingles are a North American-manufactured asphalt roofing product available in entry-level three-tab, mid-grade architectural (laminate), and premium designer styles. The brand is one of the longest-running shingle manufacturers on the continent, and their [Landmark series](https://www.certainteed.com/landmark) is among the most widely installed architectural shingles in the country. If a roofer in the Texas Panhandle is quoting you shingles, CertainTeed comes up often — here is what you need to know before you sign anything.

## The Main CertainTeed Shingle Lines

CertainTeed organizes their residential shingles into a few distinct families, each aimed at a different budget and performance level.

**Landmark Series** — This is their workhorse. The Landmark architectural shingle is a dual-layer laminate that gives you the shadow-line and dimensional look of wood shake at an asphalt price point. The family runs from standard Landmark up through Landmark Pro, Landmark TL (a heavier triple-laminate), and Landmark ClimateFlex, which uses polymer-modified asphalt to hit Class 4 UL 2218 impact resistance.

**NorthGate Series** — NorthGate and [NorthGate ClimateFlex](https://www.certainteed.com/products/residential-roofing-products/northgate-climateflex) are built specifically for impact resistance. Both carry a Class 4 UL 2218 rating — the highest classification for asphalt shingles — using a polymer-modified asphalt blend that holds together better when hailstones hit it.

**Designer / Luxury Lines** — Presidential Shake TL, Belmont, and Grand Manor sit at the top of the CertainTeed catalog. These are heavier, thicker shingles designed to mimic natural slate or wood shake. They cost more and require a steeper pitch minimum, but they add meaningful curb appeal and carry strong warranty terms.

## Why Class 4 Impact Resistance Matters in the Texas Panhandle

Amarillo sits in the middle of what weather researchers call Hail Alley. The area logs 8 to 12 hailstorms per year on average, with 131 confirmed hail-producing storm days recorded. The largest stone documented near Amarillo measured 4.25 inches — softball-sized — in May 2019.

Standard asphalt shingles take a beating in those conditions. Class 4 shingles like CertainTeed's NorthGate or Landmark ClimateFlex use a stiffer, polymer-modified asphalt layer that resists cracking and fracturing on impact. The UL 2218 test drops a 2-inch steel ball from 20 feet onto a conditioned shingle — Class 4 means it passed that test without cracking.

Beyond durability, the practical reason to consider Class 4 shingles here is insurance. Many Texas carriers offer a premium discount — sometimes 15% to 30% — when a home is re-roofed with Class 4 material. You will need to confirm with your specific insurer, but it is worth asking before you finalize a material choice. A [professional roof inspection](/roof-inspections-amarillo/) after installation can document the shingle class for your insurer's records.

## The CertainTeed Integrity Roof System and Warranty

A shingle is only part of what goes on a roof. CertainTeed's [Integrity Roof System](https://www.certainteed.com/inspiration/how-tos/certainteed-integrity-roof-system) pairs their shingles with matched underlayment (DiamondDeck or RoofRunner), ice-and-water shield (WinterGuard at eaves and valleys), SwiftStart starter strips, and compatible ridge ventilation. Using all CertainTeed components on the same job unlocks the SureStart PLUS warranty — an extended coverage option that can reach up to 50 years and covers both materials and labor, not just the shingle itself.

The catch: you only get SureStart PLUS if your installer holds the CertainTeed ShingleMaster credential. That requires completing CertainTeed's installation training, keeping at least one Master Craftsman-certified applicator on staff, and signing a code of ethics. It is a real verification, not a rubber stamp — ask any contractor you are evaluating whether they hold it before you get a quote.

## How to Choose the Right CertainTeed Shingle for Your Home

For most [asphalt shingle replacements in Amarillo](/asphalt-shingle-roofing-amarillo/), the decision comes down to three things:

1. **Budget** — Landmark standard is the most cost-effective entry point. Landmark Pro steps up the weight and warranty. NorthGate costs more but may pay back in insurance savings and reduced storm-related repairs over time.
2. **Hail exposure** — If your neighborhood has been hit in the last few years, Class 4 is worth the conversation. Ask your contractor to pull the hail history for your specific address — that data exists and it affects the math.
3. **Warranty intent** — If you plan to own the home long-term, the full Integrity Roof System with a credentialed installer makes the warranty much more meaningful. If you are prepping to sell in two years, a standard Landmark with SureStart base coverage may be sufficient.

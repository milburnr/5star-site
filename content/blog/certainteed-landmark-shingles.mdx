CertainTeed Landmark shingles are a laminated architectural asphalt shingle built with a dual-layer fiberglass design that gives them a dimensional, shadow-line look instead of the flat profile of older 3-tab shingles. They're one of the most widely installed shingle lines in the country, and for good reason: the extra layer means more mass and durability per square than a basic 3-tab, without pushing into premium-tier pricing.

## What's Actually in a Landmark Shingle

The dual-layer construction is the core of what makes Landmark different from entry-level shingles. Two fiberglass-reinforced layers are laminated together, which adds both visual depth and a meaningful bump in impact and wind resistance compared to single-layer shingles. Each shingle carries an Underwriters Laboratories Class A fire rating, and CertainTeed's [NailTrak nailing-zone technology](https://www.certainteed.com/products/residential-roofing-products/landmark) marks exactly where fasteners should go, which cuts down on installation errors that cause premature failures.

Standard Landmark shingles come with a [lifetime limited transferable warranty, a 25-year StreakFighter algae-resistance warranty](https://www.certainteed.com/products/residential-roofing-products/landmark) using copper-infused granules to fight black streaking, and a 15-year 110 mph wind-resistance warranty (upgradable to 130 mph). CertainTeed also makes Landmark IR, a Class 4 impact-resistant variant built for hail-prone regions, and Landmark Pro/Premium tiers with heavier shingles and 30-year algae warranties.

## How to Decide If Landmark Is the Right Fit

For most homeowners replacing a standard asphalt roof, Landmark is a solid mid-tier choice: dimensional appearance, real wind and algae warranties, and a manufacturer track record that's been around for decades. Where it's worth stepping up:

- **Hail-prone areas** — look at Landmark IR (Class 4 impact rating) instead of the standard line
- **High-wind exposure** — the 130 mph upgrade or a jump to Landmark Pro's stronger wind coverage
- **Long-term ownership plans** — Landmark Pro/Premium's 30-year algae warranty outlasts the standard 25-year coverage
